# ProTech Labs

ProTech Labs is a trading name of Pro Technologies Limited, a company registered in England and Wales (No. 08184949). We build and look after software for small businesses.

## What we do

- **Odoo** — new modules and changes for Odoo Community, or Enterprise on your own server; automations, reports and document templates; moves to Odoo Community.
- **Integrations** — Odoo and the services you already use, exchanging data without copy and paste: WhatsApp Business, payment providers and banks, marketplaces.
- **Telegram bots** — bots for your customers and your team, connected to Odoo or your own system.
- **WordPress and WooCommerce plugins** — plugins built for a specific job and kept up to date.
- **Linux servers** — set-up, updates, backups and moves to another host; services under systemd or in Docker.

Every job starts with a written brief and ends with something you can use, change and get support for.
